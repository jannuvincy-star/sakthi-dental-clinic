Comprehensive dental care

Our Treatments

Explore dental services designed to support oral health, restore function and help you feel confident about your smile.

🪥

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

We offer non-surgical gum care using modern laser technology. Scaling removes plaque and tartar buildup. For more advanced periodontal concerns, deep cleaning such as root planing may be recommended, supported by laser treatment when appropriate.

✨

Tooth Filling

We restore teeth affected by cavities, trauma or wear using quality filling materials and modern techniques. Our aim is to seal damaged areas, protect against further decay and preserve natural tooth structure.

🦷

Tooth Extraction

When a tooth cannot be restored, our dental team evaluates available options and performs removal with careful techniques. We explain each step and provide guidance for recovery.

😁

Artificial Complete Denture

Custom complete dentures can help restore appearance and everyday function for people missing all teeth in an arch. Each denture is designed for fit, comfort and a natural-looking smile.

⚙️

Dental Implants

Dental implants use biocompatible materials such as titanium to create a stable foundation for replacement teeth. They can restore appearance and chewing function following assessment by an implant specialist.

🔦

Laser Dentistry

Laser technology can support precise, minimally invasive procedures, including selected soft-tissue and gum treatments. Suitability depends on the individual treatment plan.

🦷

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal treatment addresses infection inside the tooth. The infected tissue is removed, the area is cleaned and sealed to help relieve pain and preserve the natural tooth.

🦷

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Impacted or problematic wisdom teeth may need removal if they cause pain, infection or damage to nearby teeth. We provide assessment, treatment and post-operative care guidance.

😁

Fixed Partial Denture (Bridge)

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by attaching a prosthetic tooth to adjacent natural teeth or implants. It can help restore appearance and chewing function.

🌟

Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)

Professional whitening may improve certain types of tooth discoloration using clinically selected bleaching agents. A dental assessment helps determine the safest suitable option.

💎

Veneers

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of teeth to improve the appearance of chips, gaps or discoloration. Treatment suitability is assessed individually.

🧸

Pediatric Dentistry

We provide gentle dental care for children, including routine check-ups and preventive guidance, in a welcoming environment designed to help young patients feel comfortable.

🩺

Flap Surgery

For selected advanced gum conditions, flap surgery allows a dentist to access and clean deeper areas around the teeth before repositioning the gum tissue for healing.

😁

Orthodontic Braces

Orthodontic treatment can correct misaligned teeth and bite concerns. Options may include metal, ceramic or lingual braces, depending on clinical needs and preferences.

🌿

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are transparent, removable trays that gradually move teeth. They offer a discreet alternative to braces for suitable cases following orthodontic assessment.
